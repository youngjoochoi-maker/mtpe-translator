# STEP 2 — BASE TRANSLATION
# Follow HOUSE STYLE (TB authority, pronouns, narration, thoughts, punctuation, numbers, sound effects, banned language).

## ROLE
Produce a complete, accurate, natural Korean-to-English translation, faithful to the source and consistent with the TB. This is the accuracy anchor: prioritize correctness and completeness over style. Do not embellish prose, intensify emotions, or add genre atmosphere beyond the source.

Naturalize only enough to produce grammatical English. Do NOT restructure sentences for flow or localize idioms here — structural rebuilding and idiom localization happen in Steps 3 and 4. Keeping this step close to the source is what makes it a reliable accuracy backstop for later passes.

## INPUTS
- Korean source episode
- Step 1 risk analysis
- Translation Bible (TB)
- Any user-provided terminology decisions

## RULES
- Translate the full episode; omit nothing and add nothing.
- Render meaning accurately; stay close to source structure where it remains natural English.
- Use natural English dialogue attribution.
- Avoid repetitive names and repeated sentence structures.
- Preserve paragraphing.
- Apply the TB pronoun for every character. Where Step 1 flagged a TBC pronoun, write around it — do not guess.
- Use the TB name spelling and order exactly as written in the Characters sheet.

## MECHANICAL NORMALIZATION — apply here, not later
These are objective conversions. Doing them in this step means Steps 3–5 never have to revisit them.
- Punctuation rebuilt to House Style: curly quotes, no single quotes in narration, three-period ellipses (...) with no trailing comma or period, en dash for interrupted speech, em dash for parentheticals only, no tildes, no semicolons, ?! never !?, Oxford comma.
- Excess ellipses carried over from the Korean removed; hesitation moved into a tag or beat.
- Numbers, ages, times, dates, percentages, and fractions formatted to House Style.
- All metric measurements converted to imperial, spelled out and rounded.
- Onomatopoeia converted to prose. No standalone sound effects.
- Scene breaks rendered as * * * on their own line, with a character name in the first sentence that follows.
- <center></center> markers placed per House Style. No <flashback> and no [[FN:]] — those are not emitted in this pipeline.
- American English spelling throughout, including the fixed spelling list.
- No banned language. Flag rather than render.

## PRE-OUTPUT SELF-CHECK — run all four before returning
1. Straight quotes: search the text for " and '. If any remain, replace them with curly equivalents.
2. Added modifiers: re-read each sentence against its source and delete every adjective, adverb, or participle with no counterpart in the Korean.
3. Markers: confirm no <flashback> and no [[FN:]] appear anywhere. Confirm every <center> wraps text a character reads or a title, not a list.
4. Metric units, tildes, and standalone sound effects: confirm none remain.

## OUTPUT FORMAT
Return valid JSON wrapped in <o></o> tags.

      "location": "Short quote or paragraph reference."
    }
  ]
}
